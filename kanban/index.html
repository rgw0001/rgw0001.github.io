A single-file Kanban board for the job search. This demo is seeded with fictional companies and data. Everything lives in your browser (localStorage); use Export / Import to back up or share.

Categories

Rename a category, move it left or right, or delete it. On the board you can also drag a category by its header, or double-click its name to rename it. Stage tells the sidebar how to count those cards, and where the group titles and dashed dividers fall.
